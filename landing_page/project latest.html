Six protocols, checked against indexed records only. This is the rubric, not a score.

Delivery against commitments

Milestones, deadlines and SLAs met as agreed, with slips explained on record.

Measurable business outcomes

Revenue, cost, client or service results that can be traced to the person's work.

03

Role-specific output

The core work of the job: shipped code, campaigns, closes, analyses, designs, contracts.

04

Cross-functional contribution

Work done with or for other departments, visible in shared project records.

05

Supporting others

06

No unresolved concerns
